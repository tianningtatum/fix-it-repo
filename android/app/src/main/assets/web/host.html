What it trains (keep this to yourself until the debrief)

  • Operator describing what they see: precise technical language under time pressure.
  • Manual holders giving instructions: being clear, checking the operator understood, and saying "that's not on my page."
  • Split manual: knowing who holds which information and asking for help fast.
  • Rotating operator: every member has to lead at least once; the team has to support quieter members.
  • Mistakes: trust vs. verify. Acting before the instruction is clear costs a strike.

Debrief questions

    Technopreneurship link

    • The split manual is a cross-functional team: nobody has the whole picture.
    • The operator is the founder facing the market, acting on what mentors and data say without seeing it all.
    • The rotating lead is shared leadership: a startup where only one person can lead is fragile.
