Fix-It Crew

One of you sees a broken machine. Everyone else holds a piece of the manual. Talk it through.

Your program
How to play
  1. Everyone on your team picks the same program and types the same team code.
  2. Each round, one phone is the operator and sees a broken machine. The others each hold different pages of the manual.
  3. The operator describes what they see. Whoever has the right page says what to do.
  4. The operator changes every round. Your team has 3 spare parts. A failed round uses one.
